# Student guide

Graduation Tracker shows how far you are from graduating and what is left. It is **advisory**: the Registry confirms who graduates. This is an independent prototype, not an official Ashesi University service.

## Signing in

- Web: https://ashesi-graduation-tracker.onrender.com. Or use the Android app.
- Enter your **student ID** and password. If the Registry gave you a temporary password, you'll be asked to choose a new one straight away (at least 12 characters, using three of: lowercase, uppercase, digits, symbols).
- Forgotten password? Ask the Registry for a temporary one.

## What each screen shows

| Screen | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Your credits completed against your programme total, your standing, your expected graduation term and your next three steps |
| **Requirements** | Every requirement for your programme and track, grouped by area. Filter by Completed, In progress, Below pass mark, Incomplete or Remaining, or search for a course |
| **Remaining** (web) / **Path to graduation** (app) | What's left: this term's courses, anything to retake or resolve, then everything still to complete before your graduation term |
| **Courses** | Every course by term, with your grade and Passed or Below pass mark. Retakes show your earlier attempts |
| **Inbox** | Messages from the Registry. Unread messages are marked, and the menu shows how many |
| **Profile** | Your programme, year group and track, and **Change password** |

## Standing

| Standing | Means |
|---|---|
| ✓ **On track** | What remains fits your remaining terms at a normal load |
| ! **At risk** | Something needs attention, such as a failed course to retake, an incomplete, or more than a normal load each term |
| ✕ **Critical** | Finishing on time would need more than the maximum load, or several required courses are below their pass marks. Talk to your advisor |

## Pass marks

Most courses pass at **D+**. Some may need a **C**; the pass mark is shown next to each course. A course below its pass mark must be retaken; your latest passing attempt is the one that counts.

## If something looks wrong

Your details and grades come from the Registry. If anything is wrong, contact the Registry; you can't change it in the app.

## Mobile app extras

- **Offline:** the app shows your last status when you have no connection, with an "Offline" banner.
- **Unlock with Face ID or fingerprint:** Profile → Unlock and password. It asks when the app opens and after a minute away.
- Signing out removes your saved data from the phone.
